# Troubleshooting
Use this topic to resolve problems.

A first degree black belt is called ShoDan. ShoDan translates to first step. Traditionally once you have earned the title of ShoDan, you know enough to begin learning karate. So, you have taken the first step.

## Question 1
<details>
<summary>Why can't I push my changes?</summary>
 
Make sure you:
 
1. Have committed your changes.
2. Are connected to the correct remote repository.
3. Have permission to push to the repository.
 
</details>

## Question 2
<details>
<summary>Why can't I commit my changes?</summary>
 
Make sure you:
 
1. Have added your changes to the staging area
2. Make sure you are in the repository directory
 
</details>
